The Surgical Solution

People-Weighted Priority
at Surgical Precision

Instead of building costly city-wide bus lanes, PulsePriority identifies the exact intersections where intervention yields the greatest human-time savings — and applies dynamic, targeted priority only there.

  • People over vehicles. A tram carrying 180 passengers scores far higher than an empty bus — delay is weighted by actual ridership.
  • Dynamic scoring. The Pulse Score recalculates in real-time as transit vehicles approach intersections, adapting to live conditions.
  • Surgical interventions. Queue-jump lanes and extended green phases deploy only at the 2–3 intersections that matter most, right now.
  • Congestion-aware. High car volumes lower the Pulse Score — avoiding interventions that would gridlock surrounding traffic.
People-Weighted Scoring Concept
Ps = (Passengers × Delay × Routes × Risk) / (Base + Cars × 0.1)
The Pulse Score Algorithm — higher = intervene first
